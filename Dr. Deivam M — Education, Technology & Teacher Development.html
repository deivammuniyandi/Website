Computer scientist turned teacher educator

I began in computer science, with a B.Sc. and M.Sc. from Madurai Kamaraj University, and moved into education at The Gandhigram Rural Institute, where I completed my M.Ed., M.Phil. and Ph.D. That mix still shapes my work: I look at teaching and learning through both a technical and a social lens.

Today I teach in the Department of Education at the S.R.T. Campus of H.N.B. Garhwal University, a Central University in Tehri Garhwal, Uttarakhand. I am qualified through UGC-NET and TN-SET in Education.

Completed research project

Transforming Education: AI-assisted self-directed learning and the digital divide in the Garhwal region

This Indian Council of Social Science Research project explored how AI-assisted, self-directed learning can help bridge the digital divide, and what that means for educational equality and inclusion among learners in the Garhwal region of Uttarakhand.
